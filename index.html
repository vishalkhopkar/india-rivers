Rivers of India: an interactive map

An interactive map of more than 30,000 rivers and streams of India. Zoom in and click a river to see where it starts, where it ends, how long it is and which river it merges into. Major rivers such as the Ganga, Brahmaputra, Indus, Godavari, Krishna, Narmada, Kaveri and Mahanadi appear first, and the smaller tributaries and streams appear as you zoom in.
