# Greater Hyderabad streams: low confidence

Written 2026-10-04, with `review/hyderabad-streams.md`. Each item is something I was not sure of. Nothing here was drawn unless it passes the test; where a stream was drawn with a doubt, the doubt is stated. Positions link to openstreetmap.org.

Summary: 16 streams are **drawn with a doubt** (items 1-4), and the rest of the items are things **not drawn**.

## Drawn, with a doubt

### 1. Balkapur Nala (40336): is it a natural stream?

- **What:** The covered drain from Mehdipatnam past Masab Tank to Hussain Sagar's south-west corner at Khairatabad, 4.2 km. Named "Balkapur Nala".
- **Where:** 17.3997 N, 78.4365 E to 17.4155 N, 78.4662 E ([head](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.39967&mlon=78.43646#map=16/17.39967/78.43646), [mouth](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.41553&mlon=78.46621#map=16/17.41553/78.46621)). OSM ways [209298790](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/209298790), [209298781](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/209298781), [209298783](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/209298783).
- **For:** HMDA lists the Balkapur Nala as one of the lake's four feeder nalas, entering from the west, and this is the only mapped drain that does. It falls 542 m to 519 m and lies on a valley floor for 52% of its length (threshold 45%).
- **Against:** (a) The lake was first filled in 1562 by a "Balkapur canal" dug from the Musi, a water-supply channel, which the rule excludes; today's nala may follow that canal's line in part. (b) OSM tags 3.28 km of it as a tunnel: the course is buried and mapped from the surface. (c) OSM does not name it; the name is matched by position. (d) No HydroRIVERS line.
- **What would settle it:** A GHMC or HMDA map of the Balkapur Nala's course. If it follows the old canal on a level line it should come off; a 23 m fall in 4 km down a valley says it is the valley's own drain.
- **What I did:** Drawn and named, ending on the lake's shore.

### 2. The Ram Nagar-Nallakunta drain (40338): joined across a gap

- **What:** An unnamed OSM drain from Ram Nagar through Vidyanagar to Nallakunta, 2.4 km, joined to the Hussain Sagar Surplus Nala.
- **Where:** 17.4129 N, 78.5130 E to 17.3944 N, 78.5043 E ([head](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.41288&mlon=78.51305#map=16/17.41288/78.51305), [mouth](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.39438&mlon=78.50434#map=16/17.39438/78.50434)). OSM ways [319567755](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/319567755), [363724306](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/363724306), [363724308](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/363724308), [319567760](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/319567760), [363724302](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/363724302), [363724310](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/363724310), [319567758](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/319567758).
- **For:** Valley floor 81%, falls 507 m to 490 m, a tank on its course.
- **Against:** OSM's line stops 0.45 km short of the Surplus Nala; the last stretch is a straight line I added to the nearest point of the nala. The real outfall may be elsewhere.
- **What would settle it:** Whether this drain reaches the Surplus Nala at Nallakunta / Barkatpura, and where.
- **What I did:** Drawn, with the gap bridged.

## Not drawn

### 5. The winding channel from Ramanthapur to Nalla Cheruvu, Uppal

- **What:** An OSM waterway tagged `river`, 4.6 km, winding from near the Musi's north bank to Nalla Cheruvu (Uppal), where it would join the Nacharam tank chain.
- **Where:** 17.3925 N, 78.5530 E to 17.4046 N, 78.5745 E ([head](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.39253&mlon=78.55301#map=16/17.39253/78.55301), [mouth](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.40460&mlon=78.57454#map=16/17.40460/78.57454)). OSM ways [1488734252](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1488734252), [1488734253](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1488734253), [1488734251](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1488734251).
- **For:** OSM maps it as a river, and it winds like one.
- **Against:** It is level (480 m to 475 m), lies on a valley floor for 0% of its length, has no HydroRIVERS line (14%), and 0.4 km of it is gaps. It starts beside the Musi and ends at a tank: the pattern of an old supply channel taking river water to the tank, which the rule excludes.
- **What would settle it:** Which way it flows, and whether it is the tank's feeder channel from the Musi.
- **What I did:** Not drawn.

### 6. The drains beside and across the Musi east of Uppal

- **What:** Three OSM drains: 4.4 km and 7.9 km along the Musi's south bank, and 8.1 km from the same point south to the Chinna Musi near Batasingaram.
- **Where:** [17.3622 N, 78.7548 E](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.36224&mlon=78.75481#map=14/17.36224/78.75481) (the shared head near Pochampally), [17.3805 N, 78.6452 E](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.38045&mlon=78.64518#map=14/17.38045/78.64518).
- **For:** Tagged as drains, not canals; the southern one falls 438 m to 396 m.
- **Against:** Two of them lie along the Musi itself and one links the Musi to the Chinna Musi over the watershed: most likely irrigation channels taken off the Musi. The southern one runs downhill for only 68% of its length.
- **What I did:** Not drawn.

### 7. The Banjara Nala

- **What:** One of Hussain Sagar's four feeders (HMDA). OSM has 2.2 km of it, from KBR Park in Jubilee Hills to Banjara Lake, and nothing from there to Hussain Sagar, 2.5 km on.
- **Where:** Lowest mapped point [17.4116 N, 78.4463 E](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.41162&mlon=78.44634#map=16/17.41162/78.44634). A separate covered drain, OSM way [237977943](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/237977943) (0.8 km, Banjara Hills to Punjagutta), may be part of it or of another nala.
- **What would settle it:** The nala's course from Banjara Lake to the lake shore (HMDA says it enters from the north-west).
- **What I did:** Not drawn: the lower half would have to be invented.

### 8. The Khajaguda-Narsingi stream

- **What:** 4.3 km of OSM stream from Khajaguda's tanks to a tank at Narsingi, ending 1.5 km from the Musi with no mapped channel onward.
- **Where:** Lowest point [17.4005 N, 78.3611 E](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.40052&mlon=78.36112#map=16/17.40052/78.36112).
- **For:** Mapped as a stream, tanks at both ends; it is surely natural.
- **Against:** Joining it to the Musi means inventing 1.5 km. The next valley east (Manikonda to Ibrahim Bagh, 40350) is drawn from HydroRIVERS and may be confused with it.
- **What I did:** Not drawn.

### 9. Drains across Hussain Sagar's open water

- **What:** Two OSM drains drawn across the lake: from the Khairatabad corner along the south shore to the outlet, and from the north shore straight to the outlet (2 ways).
- **Where:** OSM ways [318598243](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/318598243), [318505359](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/318505359).
- **Against:** They are interception or diversion lines that carry dry-weather flow past the lake, not streams; with them in, the Balkapur Nala would have run across the lake to the Surplus Nala.
- **What I did:** Taken out of the network by hand. Say if the lake-crossing line should be shown.

### 11. A large HydroRIVERS river east of Choutuppal that the map does not have

- **What:** HydroRIVERS shows a 25.1 km river with a 331 km² catchment from near Choutuppal east to the Kongal, with two tributaries (21.8 km, 11 km). The CWC data has the China Vagu and the Kongal nearby but not this line.
- **Where:** [17.1938 N, 78.8479 E](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=17.19375&mlon=78.84792#map=12/17.19375/78.84792); 61% of it lies east of the box.
- **Against:** A catchment that large with no CWC river suggests HydroRIVERS routes the drainage differently from the surveyed rivers here (it may be the China Vagu or the Kongal on another line).
- **What would settle it:** Extending the data east of 78.90 E and comparing with the CWC Kongal.
- **What I did:** Not drawn, with its tributaries; outside the area.

### 12. Place texts: set by rule

- **What:** Origin and end texts name the nearest of a hand-picked list of 169 well-known suburbs and towns, or a named tank at the head. Beyond 2.5 km they say "near". I read all 114 and corrected two by hand (Fox Sagar; Narsingi).
- **Doubtful:** In the open country the nearest listed town can be 5-10 km away, so several streams share a text ("Near Chevella, west of Hyderabad" → "The Esi near Chevella, west of Hyderabad"): 86 origins begin with "Near". Tank names come from OSM as spelt there ("Mulagund Lake", "Anatula Cheruvu").
- **What would settle it:** Say which outer places should be added to the list.

### 13. The Esi's name

- **What:** The dataset's "Mosi" (3219) was renamed Esi.
- **For:** It runs through Himayat Sagar and joins the Musi at Langar Houz; Wikipedia: Himayat Sagar was built "on Esi (a tributary of Musi)".
- **Doubt:** The spelling: Esi, Easa and Isa are all in use. The Musi's own upper course is sometimes called Musa.
- **What I did:** Renamed to "Esi".
